Key facts for planning and comparing movers

Keep these general points in view as you talk with providers:

  • Scope: Confirm whether you need a home move, office relocation, employee relocation or a combination of services.
  • Timing: The time needed varies with the size and scope of the move, the addresses and the schedule you agree on. Discuss your target dates early.
  • Cost factors: The amount to be moved, distance, access at the locations and requested services can all affect a quote. Ask providers to explain what their quote includes.
  • Comparison: Look for a clear description of the work, a schedule that reflects your needs, and information about crew training and insurance.

Common moving terms are simple once someone explains them. Origin is the starting address, and destination is the address receiving the move. Full-service relocation means a broader set of moving services, while commercial relocation refers to moving a business or workplace.
