Key facts, planning, and moving terms

Moving time and cost depend on the details of the job, so there isn’t one schedule or estimate that fits every address. The size of the move, distance between locations, requested services, and whether storage is needed can all affect planning and the overall cost. Discuss those details when arranging your move rather than relying on a general figure.

A useful comparison list includes:
- Whether the provider handles your type of move, such as an apartment, house, or office.
- Which services are part of the plan and which would need separate arrangements.
- How the provider accounts for the destination, timing, and any storage needs.
- The company’s crew training and insurance information.

A few terms you may hear: “full-service relocation” means a broader moving plan, while “commercial relocation” refers to a business move. “Storage” means holding items between move dates or while other arrangements are made. Confirm the specific details of any service before you schedule it.
